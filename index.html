The Boundary Fades

In the space between heartbeats, where time pools like forgotten rain, the shinigami walk. They are the custodians of transition, guardians of the threshold that separates breath from silence.

The forest is their domain—neither living nor dead, but existing in the liminal space where reality thins. Trees that have witnessed a thousand years of human suffering stand sentinel over paths that shift with the weight of karma.

Those who enter the forest with unfinished business may find themselves walking in circles, their reflections in still water showing faces they no longer recognize.

To understand death, you must first understand the beauty of impermanence. All things are borrowed—even your next breath. — From the Scrolls of Passage

Those Who Guide the Lost

The most merciful shinigami carry lanterns of pale blue fire. Their gaze will pierce your essence and measure the weight of your regrets.

The silent guides appear as children wearing masks of birch bark. They will lead you to the crossroads where seven paths diverge. Choose wisely.

The ancient ones manifest as deer with branches for antlers and moss-covered backs. They are the oldest guardians and only appear to souls of great significance.

What to Leave Behind

At the first torii gate, leave something that once brought you joy. This lightens the spirit for the journey ahead.

At the stone basin, wash your hands and face with water that reflects no light. This cleanses the attachments that bind you to the mortal realm.

At the hollow tree marked with red thread, whisper the name you wish to be called in the next life. If the forest accepts it, you will hear it echoed back.

Never offer blood or tears. These are currencies too powerful and may attract entities that are neither guide nor guardian.

The Weight of Deeds

In the clearing where moonlight falls even at noon, souls are weighed against a feather from the wing of truth. Those who are lighter continue onward.

Those heavy with unresolved karma are bound to the trees that line the misty ravine. They become the whispers that warn travelers of dangerous paths.

The rarest souls—those who left nothing unfinished—are permitted to drink from the silver stream. They remember nothing of their mortal life but are reborn with a map of the forest etched into their palm.

Laws of Passage

  • Never look back once you've crossed the third gate
  • Speak only when addressed directly
  • Accept no gifts except from those with red-marked hands
  • Leave the forest before the seventh sunset
  • Remember: all paths eventually lead to judgment

Keeper's Observations

The forest has been unusually active since the comet's passing. More souls wander without guides, and the boundary between worlds thins with each passing night.

The ancient maple at the center has begun to weep a substance that is neither sap nor blood. Those who taste it speak of memories that do not belong to them.

Forbidden Knowledge

There are whispers of a hidden path that circumvents judgment entirely. Those who find it may return to the world of the living, but at a cost that few would willingly pay.

The shinigami themselves are bound by laws older than the forest. They too were once travelers on these paths.

May your passage be swift and your judgment merciful
